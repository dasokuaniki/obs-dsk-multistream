# Simulcast guidelines

Last reviewed: 2026-07-31

DSK Multistream sends separate outputs to services selected by the user. The
user remains responsible for the account, creator, partner, music, copyright,
advertising, and simulcast rules that apply to each destination.

## Twitch

Twitch currently permits simulcasting, subject to its Simulcasting Guidelines.
In particular, the Twitch experience must not be worse than the experience on
another service, the streamer must not direct the Twitch community away to watch
elsewhere, and the Twitch broadcast must not display combined cross-platform
activity such as merged chat.

DSK Comment Viewer may be used as an operator-only OBS dock. Do not place a
cross-platform merged-chat or cross-platform activity overlay into the video
sent to Twitch. Review the current
[Twitch Terms of Service, Simulcasting section](https://www.twitch.tv/p/terms-of-service#simulcasting)
before each public workflow change.

## Kick

Kick permits multistreaming, but Partner Program members must enable the
Multistreaming toggle in the Kick Creator Dashboard for the relevant broadcast.
Kick states that Partner Program payout is reduced while the toggle is enabled.
The exact eligibility and payout terms may change; review Kick's current
[Partner Program multistreaming instructions](https://help.kick.com/en/articles/11091744-multistreaming-on-the-kick-partner-program).

## YouTube

YouTube access is subject to the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and
[YouTube API Services policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies).
DSK requires the user to select or confirm the intended broadcast before sending
RTMP when multiple broadcasts share a reusable stream key. The archive-rotation
option creates and transitions additional broadcasts; the user should verify
titles, visibility, audience setting, and notifications before enabling it.

## Other and Manual RTMP destinations

Manual RTMP does not imply platform approval. Confirm that the destination lets
the account simulcast and that the stream includes only content for which the
user has the required rights. TikTok is not part of the public supported-platform
branding or login flow; any personal Manual RTMP use remains the user's own
configuration.
